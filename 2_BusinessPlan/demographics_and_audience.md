# Demographics & Audience Insights

Understanding your audience is critical for marketing and product decisions. This document provides initial personas based on our target segments for WTFManuals.com.

## Personas

### 1. DIY Dave – The Home Fixer
- **Age:** 25–45  
- **Occupation:** Mix of office and blue‑collar jobs; enjoys tinkering with electronics or home repairs on weekends.  
- **Needs:** Clear instructions, parts lists, and confidence to complete repairs safely.  
- **Pain Points:** Manuals are jargon‑heavy; he spends hours searching forums for tips.  
- **Preferred Channels:** YouTube, Reddit DIY communities, Pinterest.

### 2. Technician Tanya – The Field Service Pro
- **Age:** 30–55  
- **Occupation:** Appliance repair technician/installer or mechanics.  
- **Needs:** Quick access to accurate step‑by‑step procedures, part numbers and troubleshooting, often while on site using a phone or tablet.  
- **Pain Points:** OEM manuals are outdated; carrying paper manuals is cumbersome.  
- **Preferred Channels:** LinkedIn, trade shows, professional associations.

### 3. Creator Chris – The Technical Content Producer
- **Age:** 20–40  
- **Occupation:** YouTuber or instructor creating how‑to videos.  
- **Needs:** Comprehensive guides that can be transformed into scripts, visuals and call‑outs.  
- **Pain Points:** Researching and compiling complete tutorials is time‑consuming.  
- **Preferred Channels:** YouTube, TikTok, Instagram.

### 4. Enterprise Eva – The Business Customer
- **Age:** 35–60  
- **Occupation:** Documentation manager or product manager at a hardware company.  
- **Needs:** A scalable solution to upgrade hundreds of manuals; ability to brand guides for their products and train staff.  
- **Pain Points:** Legacy manuals are scattered; manual updates are expensive.  
- **Preferred Channels:** Industry conferences, LinkedIn, direct sales.

## Demographic Insights
- **Geography:** Target primarily North America and Europe initially due to high DIY and maker culture; plan to expand globally.  
- **Language:** English first; consider Spanish, German, and French expansions.  
- **Device Usage:** Mobile and tablet usage is high among technicians; ensure responsive design and offline capability.  

## Behavioural Insights
- DIYers value community endorsements—include testimonials and curated forum tips.  
- Technicians prioritise time savings—highlight search and quick navigation features.  
- Creators need high quality visuals—emphasise script export and multimedia integration.  
- Enterprises require security, customization and integration—build enterprise plan messaging accordingly.
