# Marketing Strategy – WTFManuals.com

This document outlines the marketing tactics and campaigns to attract users to WTFManuals.com and convert them into paying customers. Our goal is to build trust, demonstrate value, and establish WTFManuals as the go‑to resource for enriched technical manuals.

## 1. Brand Positioning
- **Unique Selling Point (USP):** WTFManuals is the only platform that enriches existing manuals with real‑world tips and automatically produces interactive guides and video scripts.  
- **Tone:** Helpful, transparent, and empowering; we demystify technical tasks so users feel confident.  
- **Audience Segments:** DIYers (home owners, hobbyists), professional technicians/mechanics, video content creators, and B2B partners (manufacturers and repair shops).

## 2. Channels & Tactics

### Content Marketing
- **Blog Articles:** Publish weekly how‑to articles, repair tips, and success stories using our AI. Use targeted keywords to rank on search engines and share posts across social channels.  
- **Video Tutorials:** Create YouTube and TikTok videos demonstrating the transformation of a confusing manual into a clear step‑by‑step guide. Cross‑promote with influencers in the maker community.  
- **Technical Webinars:** Host webinars on topics like "How to Build a Better Manual" to engage professionals and collect leads.

### SEO
- Perform keyword research using tools like Ahrefs or Moz. Target long‑tail keywords such as “DIY air conditioner repair manual”, “fix [product] manual”, and “technical troubleshooting guide.”  
- Optimise on‑page SEO with descriptive titles, meta descriptions, alt text and internal linking.  
- Acquire backlinks through guest posts, partnerships and digital PR.

### Social Media & Community
- **LinkedIn:** Share product updates, blog posts and industry insights. Connect with technical writers, product managers and manufacturing leaders.  
- **Reddit & Forums:** Participate in r/DIY, r/FixIt, and product‑specific forums. Provide genuine help and link to WTFManuals when relevant (avoid spamming).  
- **Facebook Groups:** Join niche communities around home repair and share relevant guides.  
- **Twitter/X & Threads:** Announce new features, share quick tips and engage with tech influencers.

### Partnerships & Collaborations
- Partner with hardware manufacturers and e‑commerce retailers to offer enriched manuals with their products.  
- Collaborate with tool brands and maker clubs to sponsor events and workshops.  
- Offer co‑branded guides for partners’ products.

### Paid Advertising (Low‑Cost Pilot)
- **Search Ads:** Run Google Ads targeting high‑intent keywords; start with small budgets and optimise for conversion.  
- **Social Ads:** Test targeted Facebook and LinkedIn ads promoting the free tier or webinar sign‑ups.  
- **Retargeting:** Use pixel retargeting to re‑engage website visitors with reminders to complete sign up.

## 3. Conversion & Retention
- **Freemium Tier:** Offer a free plan that allows limited manual processing and encourages sign up to the paid tier.  
- **Email Nurture Sequences:** After sign up, send a welcome email, followed by educational content highlighting success stories and features. Provide periodic tips and product updates.  
- **On‑boarding Guides:** Provide interactive tutorials inside the product and link to our knowledge base to ease adoption.  
- **Referral Program:** Incentivise existing customers to refer new users by offering free credits or discounts.  
- **Customer Success:** Keep in touch with users through surveys and webinars to gather feedback and reduce churn.

## 4. Metrics & KPIs
- **Website Traffic:** Monitor organic and paid visits (Google Analytics).  
- **Lead Generation:** Track sign ups, demo requests and webinar registrations.  
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Evaluate the cost per lead and per customer.  
- **Activation Rate:** Measure the percentage of users who process their first manual.  
- **Retention & Churn:** Track renewal rates and reasons for churn to inform product improvements.

## 5. Budget & Timeline
- Start with a three‑month test period allocating 40% of the marketing budget to content creation, 30% to paid ads, and 30% to partnerships and events.  
- Evaluate the ROI and reallocate budget based on performance metrics.

## 6. Tips & Best Practices
- **Provide value first:** People follow brands that teach them something. Prioritise education and helpful content over direct selling.  
- **Focus on community:** Engage authentically in communities—avoid spamming.  
- **Iterate quickly:** Use A/B testing on landing pages, ad copy and email subject lines.  
- **Be consistent:** Maintain a consistent schedule for posting and emailing to build trust.

Use this document as a living playbook—update it as you learn from your marketing experiments and customer feedback.
