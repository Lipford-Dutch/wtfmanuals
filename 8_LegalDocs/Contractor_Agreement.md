# Independent Contractor Agreement

**This Agreement** is made effective as of {EffectiveDate}, by and between **{CompanyName}** (“Company”) and **{ContractorName}** (“Contractor”).

## 1. Services
Contractor agrees to perform the following services for Company: {DescriptionOfServices}. Contractor will determine the method and manner of performing the services.

## 2. Compensation
Company will pay Contractor {FeeAmount} for the services. Contractor shall invoice Company according to the schedule in Exhibit A. Company will reimburse pre‑approved expenses upon receipt of documentation.

## 3. Independent Contractor Status
Contractor is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employer‑employee, joint venture or partnership relationship. Contractor is responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits associated with its personnel.

## 4. Confidentiality and IP
Contractor will maintain the confidentiality of Company’s proprietary information. All deliverables created under this Agreement shall be considered “work made for hire” and the intellectual property shall vest in Company. Contractor assigns all rights, title and interest in such deliverables to Company.

## 5. Term and Termination
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the services are completed or terminated. Either party may terminate with {TerminationNotice} days’ written notice. Upon termination, Company shall pay Contractor for services rendered through the termination date.

## 6. Indemnification
Contractor will indemnify and hold Company harmless from any claims arising from Contractor’s performance of the services, except to the extent caused by Company’s negligence.

## 7. Governing Law
This Agreement will be governed by the laws of {GoverningLawJurisdiction}. Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of that jurisdiction.

## 8. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.
