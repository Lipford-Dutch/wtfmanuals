# Employment Agreement

**This Employment Agreement** (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of {EffectiveDate} by and between **{CompanyName}** (the “Company”) and **{EmployeeName}** (the “Employee”).

## 1. Position and Duties
The Company hereby employs Employee as {PositionTitle}. Employee agrees to perform the duties and responsibilities customary to this position and as directed by the Company. Employee shall devote full working time and attention to the Company.

## 2. Compensation
Employee will receive an annual salary of {SalaryAmount} payable in accordance with the Company’s standard payroll practices. Employee may also be eligible for bonuses or equity awards as determined by the Company.

## 3. Benefits
Employee will be entitled to participate in Company benefit plans, including health insurance, retirement plans, and paid time off, subject to the terms of those plans.

## 4. Term and Termination
- Employment is at will. Either party may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice.  
- Upon termination, Employee will be entitled to compensation and benefits earned up to the termination date.  

## 5. Confidentiality & IP Assignment
Employee acknowledges that during employment they will have access to confidential information and agrees to keep such information confidential. Employee assigns to the Company any inventions or works of authorship created within the scope of employment.

## 6. Non‑Competition and Non‑Solicitation
For {NonCompetePeriod} after termination, Employee will not compete with the Company in {RestrictedTerritory}. For {NonSolicitPeriod} after termination, Employee will not solicit the Company’s employees or customers.

## 7. Dispute Resolution
Any disputes arising from this Agreement will be resolved by arbitration in {Jurisdiction} under the rules of {ArbitrationInstitution}.

## 8. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of {GoverningLawJurisdiction}.

## 9. Entire Agreement
This Agreement, together with any exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding between the parties. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
