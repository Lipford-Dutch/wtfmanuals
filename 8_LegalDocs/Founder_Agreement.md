## 5. Non‑Competition and Non‑Solicitation
During employment with the Company and for {NonCompetePeriod} years thereafter, each Founder shall not engage in a business that competes with the Company. For {NonSolicitPeriod} years after leaving the Company, each Founder shall not solicit employees or customers of the Company.

If a Founder leaves the Company voluntarily or is terminated for cause before full vesting, unvested shares shall be forfeited. If a Founder leaves involuntarily without cause, unvested shares may be repurchased by the Company at the original purchase price.
