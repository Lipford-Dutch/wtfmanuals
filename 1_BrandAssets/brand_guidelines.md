# WTFManuals.com – Brand Guidelines

Welcome to your brand kit. Use this document to ensure all materials for WTFManuals.com remain consistent, professional, and memorable.

## Brand Essence
- **Name:** WTFManuals.com ("Where Technical Fixes" Manuals).  
- **Tagline:** "Turn chaos into clarity."  
- **Mission:** Empower DIYers and technicians with AI‑enhanced manuals that blend official instructions with real‑world insights.

## Visual Identity

### Logo
- The primary logo is provided in `logo.png`. It features an abstract geometric design inspired by technology and instruction manuals.  
- Use the logo on a white or very light background for maximum contrast. Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "W" around the logo.

### Color Palette
- **Primary Color – Deep Blue:** `#0E1F4E` (trustworthy, professional).  
- **Accent Color – Bright Red:** `#E63946` (attention‑grabbing, energetic).  
- **Secondary Neutral – Light Gray:** `#F5F5F5` (backgrounds, panels).  
- **Secondary Dark – Slate:** `#273145` (secondary text, footers).

When using colors together, ensure sufficient contrast for accessibility. Use the deep blue for headings and primary buttons, the bright red for call‑to‑action elements, and neutrals for backgrounds.

### Typography
- **Headings:** Montserrat Bold, all caps.  
- **Body text:** Open Sans Regular.  
- **Monospace:** Fira Code or Courier New for code snippets or part numbers.  

If Montserrat or Open Sans are unavailable, substitute with system sans‑serif fonts (Helvetica, Arial).

### Tone & Voice
- **Confident and friendly:** Write in a clear, conversational manner, avoiding corporate jargon.  
- **Helpful and empowering:** Focus on solving users’ problems and guiding them through complex tasks.  
- **Transparent:** Explain reasoning behind recommendations.  

### Iconography & Imagery
- Use simple line icons for tools, documents, and technology.  
- Favor photos that show real people using tools or working on devices.  
- Avoid overly stocky or generic imagery.  

### Do’s and Don’ts
-  Do maintain consistent color usage and spacing.
-  Do keep messaging centred on user empowerment.
-  Don’t crowd the logo or stretch it.
-  Don’t overuse the accent colour; reserve it for CTAs.

## Using These Assets
The `logo.png` file in this folder can be placed on your website, documents, presentations and slides. For print, export at 300 dpi. Always maintain aspect ratio when resizing.
