# Financial Forecast Assumptions

The financial forecast (see `financial_forecast.csv`) is based on conservative assumptions about customer growth, pricing, and operational costs. Adjust these figures based on actual traction and expenses.

## Revenue Assumptions
- **Subscription Pricing:** Average subscription plan priced at $25/month per user, with an expected retention rate of 85%.  
- **User Growth:** Start with 500 active users in Year 1; project 3× growth in Year 2 and 2× growth in Year 3 driven by marketing and partnerships.  
- **Professional Services:** Revenue from custom manual conversions and enterprise integrations grows gradually as the user base expands.

## Expense Assumptions
- **Infrastructure:** Cloud hosting and AI services cost ~20% of revenue initially, decreasing to ~15% as scale improves efficiency.  
- **Salaries:** Early team of five increases to ten by Year 3; salary expenses included in operating expenses.  
- **Marketing:** 15–20% of revenue allocated to marketing and advertising.  
- **General & Administrative:** Insurance, accounting, legal fees, software licences and rent.

## Profit Estimation
- Profit is calculated as total revenue minus operating expenses.  
- Assumes reinvestment of a portion of profits into product R&D and marketing.  
- No taxes accounted in this simple model; consult a CPA for net profit calculations.

## Funding Needs
- The projection does not include future fundraising. The business may require additional capital to scale more quickly or to expand internationally.

Use this as a starting point and refine as more data becomes available.
