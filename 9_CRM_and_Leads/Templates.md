# Outreach & Nurture Templates

Use the following templates for your cold outreach, LinkedIn messages, and nurture sequences. Remember to personalise names and companies, and adjust the tone to suit your brand.

## Cold Email Template
```
Subject: Transform your manuals into interactive guides with AI

Hi {FirstName},

I came across your work at {Company} and noticed your product manuals could benefit from better clarity.

At WTFManuals.com we use AI to enrich existing manuals with community‑sourced tips and produce interactive guides and video scripts. Our clients have reduced support calls by 30%.

Would you be open to a quick call next week to see how we can help improve your documentation?

Thanks,
{YourName}
Founder, WTFManuals.com
```

## LinkedIn DM Template
```

```
