# Lead Scoring and Pipeline Management

A simple lead scoring model helps prioritise outreach based on the likelihood of conversion. This document suggests a basic approach tailored for WTFManuals.com.

## Lead Stages
1. **New:** Contact has expressed initial interest (e.g., downloaded a guide or submitted a form).  
2. **Qualified:** Lead has engaged with your content (e.g., attended a webinar, responded to an email) and matches your ideal customer profile.  
3. **Customer:** Lead has purchased a subscription or contracted for services.

## Scoring Criteria (0–100 points)
| Criterion | Points | Notes |
|-----------|-------|------|
| Job title/role relevant to technical documentation or repair | +20 | Documentation managers, service technicians, product managers. |
| Company size (>50 employees) | +10 | Indicates budget for enterprise subscription. |
| Engagement level (downloads, clicks, webinar attendance) | +5 each | Add 5 points for each action. |
| Responses to outreach emails | +15 | Indicates active interest. |
| Industry match (hardware, manufacturing, DIY) | +15 | Aligned industries are more likely to convert. |
| Negative signals (unsubscribe, no response after 3 follow‑ups) | −10 | May need to lower priority. |

### Score Thresholds
- **0–39:** Cold – Low priority. Add to nurture sequences and monitor.  
- **40–69:** Warm – Ready for personalised outreach or demo.  
- **70–100:** Hot – High likelihood to convert. Prioritise one‑to‑one engagement.

## Pipeline Management Tips
- Regularly review and update lead scores based on new activity.  
- Use Notion’s formula and filter features to compute scores automatically.  
- Assign leads above the Warm threshold to sales/outreach team members.  
- For leads that remain Cold for more than 60 days, consider a targeted re‑engagement campaign or archive them.  

## Best Practices
- Keep criteria simple; too many factors make scoring complex and inaccurate.  
- Align scoring with your business goals. For example, if you’re focusing on enterprise customers, weigh company size more heavily.  
- Continuously refine your model based on closed‑won and closed‑lost analysis. Identify patterns among high‑scoring leads that convert vs. those that do not.  

A basic scoring system like this can significantly increase the efficiency of your outreach and help your team focus on the most promising opportunities.
