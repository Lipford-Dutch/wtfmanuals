# Lead Generation Strategy

This document outlines a cost‑effective lead generation strategy tailored for WTFManuals.com. Use it to consistently attract new prospects and feed them into your Notion CRM.

## 1. Channels

### 1.1 Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)
- Optimise your website for keywords related to DIY manuals, troubleshooting, and tech repair.  
- Regularly publish helpful blog posts and tutorials targeting long‑tail keywords.  
- Build backlinks through guest posts on relevant blogs and participation in maker forums.

### 1.2 Content & Social Media Marketing
- **YouTube & TikTok:** Share short videos demonstrating how your platform enhances a manual. Include a call‑to‑action linking to a free trial or lead magnet.  
- **LinkedIn:** Publish thought leadership posts about AI in technical documentation and share case studies; connect with product managers and technical writers.  
- **Reddit/Forums:** Provide genuine answers on DIY threads and include subtle references to your platform. Avoid overt promotions.

### 1.3 Email Outreach
- Build a list of potential enterprise customers (e.g., documentation managers at manufacturing companies).  
- Send a personalised cold email introducing WTFManuals.com, highlighting benefits and offering a demo (see templates below).

### 1.4 Networking & Partnerships
- Attend trade shows (electronics, maker fairs, repair industry events) and gather leads via QR‑code sign ups.  
- Partner with online communities (e.g., Discord groups) to run webinars or AMAs.

### 1.5 Paid Ads (Test Phase)
- Run targeted Google search ads for high‑intent queries like “how to fix [product] manual” or “manual API service.”  
- Use Facebook and LinkedIn ads to promote your free tier; keep budgets small and test multiple creatives.

## 2. Opt‑In Offer
Offer a valuable resource in exchange for an email address:
- **Free Mini‑Guide:** “Top 10 Ways to Fix Common Appliance Issues” compiled from your enriched manuals.  
- **Free Trial:** Process one manual for free to experience the platform.  
- **Webinar Registration:** Invite leads to a live demo session explaining how to transform manuals.

Create a dedicated landing page with a clear headline, benefits, a preview of the guide or trial, and a form connected to your Notion CRM.

## 4. Landing Page Structure
- **Headline:** Clearly communicate the offer (e.g., “Free Guide: Top DIY Fixes for Home Appliances”).  
- **Bullets:** List benefits and what the user will learn.  
- **Image:** Show a snippet of the guide or a screenshot of the platform.  
- **Opt‑In Form:** Collect name and email; require minimal fields to maximise conversions.  
- **Social Proof:** Include a testimonial or trust badge.  
- **Call to Action:** “Download Now” or “Start Free Trial.”

## 5. Best Practices
- Personalise outreach messages with the recipient’s name and company.  
- Test different subject lines and track open/click rates.  
- Follow up 2–3 times if you don’t receive a reply; space these emails a few days apart.  
- Keep forms simple; the more fields you require, the lower the conversion rate.  
- Provide immediate value—send the promised guide or schedule a demo quickly.

Use this strategy as a roadmap and iterate based on results. Document what works best in your CRM notes and refine your channels and messaging over time.
